# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want PSPkinit to be a respectful, constructive place to collaborate. We welcome participation regardless of background, identity, experience, or point of view, and expect everyone to help keep project spaces safe and focused.

## Expected Conduct

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Give constructive feedback focused on ideas and work, not personal traits.
- Accept differing experiences and viewpoints in good faith.
- Respect other people's privacy and obtain permission before sharing their personal information.
- Keep project discussions relevant and professional.

## Unacceptable Conduct

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information without consent, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable. This applies to project spaces and to conduct outside them when it affects someone's participation in the project.

## Scope

This policy applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, and other spaces used for PSPkinit project activities.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. **Jake Hildreth is the primary contact**; contact him through the private contact channel listed on [his website](https://jakehildreth.com). Please do not raise sensitive conduct reports in a public issue or pull request. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Maintainers will review reports, protect the privacy of those involved, and determine an appropriate response. Responses may include a warning, removal of content, temporary restrictions, or removal from project spaces. Maintainers may decline to act on reports they determine to be made in bad faith.
